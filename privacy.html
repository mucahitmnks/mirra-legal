Privacy Policy

Mirra: AI Face Editor · Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Mirra: AI Face Editor ("Mirra", "the app"). The app is published by Mücahit Menekşe ("we", "us"), who is the data controller. Contact: mucahitmenekse1@gmail.com.

Short version

  • Makeup, eye color and blemish edits run entirely on your iPhone. Those photos never leave your device.
  • Hair, beard, age, smile and wrinkle edits are processed on our server. The photo is sent only after you explicitly agree, encrypted, used only to make your edit, and deleted right after the result is returned.
  • We never use your photos to train AI models, never sell them and never share them for advertising.
  • We have no account system, no ads and no tracking.

1. Photos

On-device features. Makeup, eye color and blemish removal are processed on your device with Apple's built-in frameworks. These photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Server features. When you use hair, beard, age, smile or wrinkle edits, the app sends the selected photo and the chosen edit to our server. Before the first upload the app asks for your explicit consent; you can withdraw it at any time in Settings, and server features then stop working until you consent again.

  • The photo is encrypted inside the app (application-level encryption on top of HTTPS) before it is sent.
  • Our server forwards it to our AI processing provider with storage turned off, receives the edited image and returns it to you.
  • Neither we nor the provider store the photo or the result after the request is finished, and neither is written to logs.
  • Photos are not used to identify you. We do not create face templates, face prints or any biometric identifiers.
  • Photos are never used to train or improve AI models.

Edited images you save go to your own Photos library and stay under your control.

2. Other data we process

  • App-generated device identifier. A random identifier created by the app, used only to count your free server edits and to prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID or any other identity. Usage counters are deleted automatically after they are no longer needed.
  • IP address. Used for a short time to limit request rates and protect the service; not stored with your photos and not used to locate or profile you.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, the app sends Apple's signed transaction to our server so we can confirm that Pro is active. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, browsing history or advertising identifier. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Service providers

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosts our server.
  • Features & Labels, Inc. (fal.ai) — runs the AI model for server edits, with storage disabled.
  • Apple — App Store payments and subscriptions, under Apple's own privacy policy.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, for users in Türkiye, your explicit consent or the safeguards allowed under KVKK Article 9.

4. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Server photo processing: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1) and Art. 9 for transfers abroad).
  • Free-edit counting, abuse prevention and subscription checks: performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK), you have the right to know whether we process your data, to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). Because we keep no photos and no account, there is usually nothing about you stored on our side. You can use Settings → Delete my data in the app, or email us; we will reply within 30 days.

6. Children

Mirra is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly process children's data.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS plus application-level encryption. Server requests without that encryption are rejected. Photos are kept in memory only while your edit is being made.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
