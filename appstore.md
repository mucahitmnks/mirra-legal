## App Review notes

Mirra edits a face photo. No login or demo account is needed.

How to test:
1. Launch, allow photo access (or use the camera), pick any photo with a clear face.
2. On-device edits (work offline, free): Makeup, Eyes, Skin → Clear blemishes.
3. Server AI edits: Hair, Beard, Age, Smile, Skin → Wrinkles. The first time, the app shows an explicit consent screen explaining that the photo is sent encrypted, processed, and deleted right after; tap the checkbox, then Continue.
4. Each device has 2 free server edits. After that the paywall appears. Use the sandbox account to buy Mirra Pro (weekly, monthly or yearly; yearly has a free trial). Restore purchases is in Settings.
5. Settings → Privacy lets the user withdraw server-processing consent; Settings → Delete my data resets local data.

Notes:
- Server edits use a third-party AI model through our server with storage disabled; photos are not stored, logged or used for training.
- Users cannot type free-text prompts; edits come from fixed presets, and unsafe results are blocked by the provider's safety filter.
- Privacy Policy and Terms links are in the paywall and in Settings.
